## 3. Scroll Wheel (persistent indicator) — updated spec

- **Appears:** at the same moment the orientation strip appears (not after — simultaneous)
- **Line:** thin black vertical line fixed to the right edge of the viewport, running only through the **middle 80%** of the edge (leaves top 10% and bottom 10% uncovered)
- **Wheel:** small black circular wheel, first appears at the **20% mark** down the line
- **Center dot:** a fixed **orange dot** at the center of the wheel (static accent — separate from the cursor-motion hover effect elsewhere on the site)
- **Numbers:** 1–6, synced to the orientation strip's numbering, displayed on the wheel and kept upright (counter-rotated against the wheel's spin)
- **On scroll:**
  - Wheel **rotates** continuously (spinning motion tied to scroll)
  - Wheel also **translates downward along the line**, tracking scroll progress from its 20% starting point toward the bottom of the line
  - Number changes with an **odometer roll** (~150–250ms), direction matching scroll direction (rolls up revealing the next number when scrolling down, reverses on scroll up)
  - Number switches when the **next section** (matching the strip's numbering) enters view — drive this off Intersection Observer per section, not raw scroll percentage
- **Mobile:** simplify to a small static progress dot or hide below a breakpoint — the right-edge wheel won't fit well on narrow screens
